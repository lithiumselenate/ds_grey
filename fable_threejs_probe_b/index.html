    HX-9 viewer help

    Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. Click a cyan marker or use the Inspection list to read about a component. States sequence the aircraft safely: the winch needs Rescue, the exploded view needs Maintenance with rotors stopped.
